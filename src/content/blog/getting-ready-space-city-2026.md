San Antonio didn't go how we wanted. So the past couple of weeks have been about exactly one thing: fixing what went wrong and making sure it doesn't happen again.

Our bumpers are fixed and fully up to spec this time — we checked, then checked again. Beyond that, we've been using the extra time to clean up everything else that was bothering us about the robot's performance on the field, not just the paperwork side of things.

Next stop is FIT District Space City in Friendswood. We're not going in trying to prove anything to anyone else. We just want to go play the way we know we're capable of.
